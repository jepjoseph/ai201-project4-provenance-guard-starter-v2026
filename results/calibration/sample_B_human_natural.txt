Long time programmer but somewhat new to react/express/javascript. The trouble is that the other site is slow and I think the react app is timeing out, its kinda of hard to debug because everytime I sit to debug it then everything works. Also I don't see anyway to set or adjust any timeouts. I could break this up into multiple smaller requests but that doesn't seem to fix the core issue of not controlling the timeout and makes the overall design worse.
