My typical school day

My school days are kinda weird because I got basically 3 different schedules, on Monday I have three lectures and wednesday and friday I have four so I’ll just use Monday for my typical day.

I wake up, take a shower and put on deodorant and what not, get nice and clean then head to my English Lecture at 10:10 in the morning.

I chill and do my work for an hour, mostly chilling because I love that class and I can handle the work nice and easy.

Then I go to my student union building after lecture and study a bit, and then get lunch and chill with the boys a lot because my next lecture that day is at 2:30 so I have a 4 hour break.

After all that I go to my Math lecture and then my programming lecture, hammer those out because I’m awesome like that. And then I go back to the student union building, chill a bit and then get dinner, then I just have a good time until 10 O’ Clock and I go right to sleep!
